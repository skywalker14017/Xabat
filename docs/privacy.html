Privacy Policy

Xabat

Last Updated: August 15, 2026

Xabat ("Xabat", "we", "us", or "our") is a Discord-based reporting and triage bot designed to help users report grooming, sexual abuse, sextortion, child endangerment, and related incidents.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Xabat collects, how it is used, who may access it, and how long it is retained.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a report, Xabat may collect:

  • Your Discord user ID.
  • Your Discord username or display name when you choose to share your identity with moderators.
  • The reported individual's online name or handle.
  • Ages provided for the reported individual and alleged victim.
  • The country or jurisdiction provided in the report.
  • The details and description you provide about the incident.
  • Images or screenshots you voluntarily submit as evidence.
  • Technical information necessary for operating the reporting system, such as report IDs and timestamps.

If you choose Stay Anonymous, your Discord identity is not displayed to moderators within the report. However, your Discord user ID is still stored internally so that Xabat can associate your report with your account and manage follow-up communication.

2. How Evidence Images Are Handled

Xabat is specifically designed to discourage the submission of explicit sexual material or Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM).

Users are instructed not to upload CSAM or explicit sexual images. Where possible, screenshots should be cropped or redacted so that only relevant conversation text and non-explicit evidence is visible.

When an image is submitted:

  1. Xabat downloads the attachment for processing.
  2. The image is temporarily stored on the bot server for automated screening.
  3. NudeNet performs automated explicit-content screening.
  4. If the image is flagged, Xabat rejects the upload and does not forward it to the Xabat moderation team or retain it as case evidence.
  5. Temporary processing data is deleted after scanning.
  6. If the image is not flagged, it is placed into a pending moderation queue where an authorized moderator can approve or delete it.

The automated scanner is a safety measure and is not guaranteed to identify every form of explicit or illegal material. Users must not rely on the scanner as permission to upload prohibited material.

3. Report Storage

Submitted reports are stored in Xabat's database so that authorized moderators can review and manage cases.

Reports may contain highly sensitive information, including allegations of sexual abuse, grooming, sextortion, and child endangerment.

Access to reports and evidence is restricted to designated administrators and authorized moderators responsible for operating the Xabat reporting system.

4. Evidence Retention

Xabat uses limited retention periods designed to avoid keeping sensitive information longer than necessary.

  • Incomplete evidence-upload sessions expire after 10 minutes of inactivity or 1 hour after creation, whichever occurs first.
  • Rate-limit information is automatically deleted after approximately 24 hours.
  • Evidence discussion threads are automatically deleted after approximately 120 days.
  • Closed reports are automatically deleted from the Xabat database after approximately 180 days.
  • Pending or active reports may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to allow the moderation team to review and resolve the case.

Deletion from Xabat's systems does not necessarily mean that copies held by third-party services, such as Discord, are simultaneously deleted. Third-party services are governed by their own privacy policies and retention practices.

5. How Information Is Used

Information submitted to Xabat is used to:

  • Receive and organize incident reports.
  • Allow authorized moderators to review reports.
  • Communicate with reporters regarding their reports.
  • Process and triage submitted evidence.
  • Prevent abuse of the reporting system.
  • Maintain report status and case-management information.
  • Investigate technical problems and maintain the service.
  • Where appropriate, facilitate reporting or escalation to relevant platforms, organizations, or authorities.

Xabat does not intentionally use submitted reports or evidence for advertising, marketing, or commercial profiling.

6. Reporting and Disclosure

Xabat may disclose information from a report when reasonably necessary to address serious safety concerns, comply with applicable law, respond to valid legal requests, or facilitate reporting of suspected criminal activity to an appropriate platform, organization, or authority.

Where appropriate, this may include relevant report information or evidence.

Xabat does not represent itself as a law-enforcement agency and does not independently determine whether a reported individual is guilty of a crime. Reports are allegations that may require further investigation by appropriate authorities.

7. Anonymous Reports

Choosing the anonymous-report option hides the reporter's Discord identity from the moderators viewing the report.

However, anonymous reporting does not mean that Xabat has no record connecting the report to the Discord account that submitted it. The Discord user ID is retained internally for report management and communication purposes.

8. Security

Xabat uses access controls, restricted Discord channels, moderator permissions, database access restrictions, automated cleanup, and evidence-screening mechanisms to reduce unauthorized access to sensitive reports.

However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Users should avoid submitting unnecessary personal information and should redact information that is not relevant to the report.

9. Third-Party Services

Xabat operates through third-party services, including Discord and hosting/infrastructure providers.

Information transmitted through these services may be processed according to their respective policies and terms.

Xabat does not control the internal storage, processing, or retention practices of third-party providers.

10. Children's Privacy

Xabat may receive reports submitted by minors because the service is intended to provide a reporting mechanism for incidents involving abuse and exploitation.

We do not intentionally collect unnecessary information about minors. Users should only provide information that is relevant to the incident being reported.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Xabat's functionality, data practices, or legal requirements change.

The current version will be made available through Xabat's official legal documentation.

Xabat is a reporting and triage tool, not a law-enforcement agency.

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact the appropriate emergency services or a relevant child-protection or law-enforcement authority.
