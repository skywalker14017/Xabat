3. Prohibited Evidence

You MUST NOT upload Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM), explicit sexual images, or other illegal sexual material to Xabat.

This prohibition exists to protect reporters, moderators, infrastructure providers, and other users.

If a conversation contains explicit imagery or CSAM, crop or redact the imagery before submitting the screenshot whenever reasonably possible. Xabat generally only needs the relevant text of the conversation to evaluate a report.

Xabat automatically screens submitted images using NudeNet before an image is forwarded to the moderation team.

The automated scanner is a safety mechanism and is not guaranteed to detect every form of explicit or illegal material. Its presence does not give users permission to submit prohibited material.

If an image is flagged, Xabat rejects the upload and does not forward it to the Xabat moderation team or retain it as case evidence. Temporary processing data used for scanning is deleted after processing.

Images that pass automated screening may be placed into a restricted moderation queue.

Authorized moderators may review submitted evidence and may:

  • Approve evidence for inclusion in a case.
  • Delete evidence.
  • Request additional information.
  • Close a report.
  • Mark a report as false or invalid where appropriate.
  • Escalate relevant information to an appropriate platform, organization, or authority.

Automated screening does not constitute a legal determination or a determination that an image is lawful.

5. Anonymous Reporting

Xabat provides an option to submit a report without displaying the reporter's Discord username to moderators.

Choosing Stay Anonymous means your Discord identity will not be displayed within the report to the moderation team.

However, anonymous reporting does not mean that Xabat has no record connecting the report to the submitting Discord account. Xabat stores the Discord user ID internally so that reports can be managed and, where necessary, the reporter can be contacted.

Choosing Share My Discord Name allows your Discord identity to be displayed alongside the report.

For additional information, see the Xabat Privacy Policy.

6. Data Retention

Active reports may remain available for as long as reasonably necessary for moderation and case handling.

These periods apply to Xabat-controlled data. Third-party services such as Discord may have separate storage and retention practices.
